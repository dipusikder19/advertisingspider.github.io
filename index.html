A few useful answers

Before we get started.

Every business is different. Here are a few things we can work through together.

Can you help with an account that is already running?

Yes. We can review the account structure, targeting, conversion setup, and current reporting, then agree on the most useful priorities.

Do you set up tracking as well as campaigns?

Yes. GA4 and Google Tag Manager are part of the service mix, so measurement can be considered alongside campaign planning and management.

How do we decide which channel to use?

We start with your audience, offer, goals, and available data. The right channel depends on where people are in their decision process and what you need to learn.

What should I share before we talk?

A short note about your business, target audience, current marketing, and the outcome you want is enough to start the conversation.
